# Fortis Support

Fortis is private, invite-only, end-to-end encrypted chat. This page is the support
contact required for the App Store listing.

**Contact:** 5jx7fcydby@privaterelay.appleid.com

We aim to respond within a few business days. Abuse reports are prioritised.

## Common questions

**I've lost a conversation.**
This is expected. Fortis stores no message history anywhere — not on our servers, not on
your device between sessions. When everyone leaves a room, the conversation is gone
permanently. There is no recovery, by design.

**I forgot my room PIN.**
PINs cannot be recovered. We never receive your PIN — only a one-way fingerprint of it —
so we have nothing to look up. Ask someone still in the room, or start a new one.

**I forgot my App Lock PIN.**
App Lock is stored only in your device's secure keychain, hashed. We cannot reset it.
Reinstalling the app clears it, but also clears your local settings.

**Someone is harassing me.**
Tap their name — on one of their messages, or in the room's member list — to Block or
Report them. Blocking hides them for the rest of that session. Reports go to us with the
reason and no message content (we cannot read message content). When enough different
people report the same device, that device is blocked from joining rooms. You can also
email the address above.

**Can you read my messages / hand them over?**
No. Messages are encrypted on your device with keys our servers never receive. This is
not a policy we could quietly change — we do not possess the data. See the
[privacy policy](privacy.md) for exactly what we can and cannot see.

**The app can't connect.**
Check your internet connection first. If it persists, email us — it may be a problem on
our end.

**Reporting a security issue.**
Please email the address above with details. We take cryptographic and privacy issues
seriously and will respond as quickly as we can.
